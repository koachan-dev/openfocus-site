OpenFocus

A minimal pomodoro that starts the moment you open it.

What it is

Open the app. A 25-minute focus timer is already counting down. When focus ends, a 5-minute break begins. When break ends, focus begins again. The cycle continues without you touching the screen.

No settings screen. No account. No subscription. Built by subtraction, not addition.

Privacy, in one sentence

OpenFocus does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Read the full privacy policy.
